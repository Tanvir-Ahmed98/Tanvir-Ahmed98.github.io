04 Publications

Featured publication

Journal Article · Oct 2021

An integrated machine learning model for indoor network optimization to maximize coverage

Ahmed Wasif Reza, Abdullah Al Rifat, Tanvir Ahmed

Indonesian Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (IJEECS), Vol. 24, No. 1, pp. 394–402 · ISSN 2502-4760

Introduces an integrated machine learning model to maximize indoor wireless coverage with a minimum number of transmitters, allocating users to the most reliable signal using K-means clustering, KNN, SVM, and Gaussian Naïve Bayes — validated with K-fold cross-validation. KNN performed best, averaging 93.33% accuracy.
